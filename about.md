# About

I'm currently wandering around various concepts in Type Theory. If you read/wrote a fun paper recently please feel free to reach out to me at [firstname].[lastname]@st-annes.ox.ac.uk.

## Research and background

In summer of 2025 I worked as an intern at ELTE under [Ambrus Kaposi](https://akaposi.github.io/) on works related to GATs. I have also worked as a research assistant with [Nobuko Yoshida](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/nobuko.yoshida/) in the Mobility Reading Group at Oxford, mechanising the metatheory of session types in Coq.

My Master's thesis with [Maximilian Doré](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/maximilian.dore/) was titled “Use It or Lose It. Resource-Indexed Types and Contractible Spaces in Substructural Dependent Type Theory.” You can usually see what kind of work I've been reading by visiting my [reading list](reading-list/index.html) and [notes page](notes-page/index.html).
